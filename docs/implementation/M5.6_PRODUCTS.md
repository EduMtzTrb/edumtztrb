## Products vs Work vs Art

Products has the most resolved, least atmospheric register of any district built so far — deliberately not a copy of Work's, Research's, Lab's, or Art's evidence hierarchy, and reasoned explicitly rather than defaulted:

- **Work** resolves and demonstrates a professional outcome — Outcome gets emphasized authority because it is the resolved *result of effort*.
- **Art** concentrates perception — evidence stays deliberately restrained and recessive (§74's "very low visible metadata").
- **Products** operationalizes value — DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §75 explicitly forbids the Art-style latency: "**transaction-critical information may not become latent**." Price and availability are the two facts a visitor needs to decide whether to keep evaluating, so they sit in `.evidence-primary` alongside the value proposition, and both additionally carry `MetadataRail`'s `tier: "primary"` — the only district so far to put transactional facts at that authority level in Metadata deliberately, matching §75's own explicit instruction rather than the Latent-leaning pattern Work/Research/Lab/Art all otherwise favor for their own metadata.

This is a genuinely different, source-justified hierarchy for a third time in Milestone 5 (Research: no emphasized field at all; Lab: Question+Observation privileged; Art: concentrated void with recessive metadata; Products: price/availability privileged and explicitly never latent) — each district's own authority document dictates a different answer to "what deserves visual authority," and the implementation follows that difference rather than imposing one shared template.
