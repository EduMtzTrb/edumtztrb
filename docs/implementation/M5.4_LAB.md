## Lab vs Research vs Work

The canonical-record evidence hierarchy is the clearest single expression of all three districts' distinct purpose, and deliberately not copied between any of them:

| District | Primary evidence (full authority) | Supporting evidence (Secondary tier) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work | Summary + **Outcome** (`type-body-large`) | Process, Context, Contribution | Work resolves and demonstrates — the resolved result earns real visual dominance. |
| Research | Summary + Question (ordinary weight) | Context, Methods, Evidence, **Position**, Uncertainty | Research asks and interprets — RESEARCH_UNIVERSE_v1.1.md §11 forbids making a hypothesis look verified, so no field gets Work's emphasis; Position is deliberately dimmed to read as provisional. |
| Lab | Summary + Question + **Observation** (ordinary weight, but privileged position) | Method, Evidence (raw), Failure note, Open edge, Lineage | Lab tests and transforms — Observation is genuinely observable evidence (LAB_UNIVERSE_v1.1.md §5's own third qualification element, non-negotiable), so it earns primary placement alongside the question it answers, without Work's false-certainty-risking type-scale emphasis (Lab's own result can just as easily be a documented failure). |

Three further concrete differences, each reasoned at its own point of use:

1. **Curated view filter.** `/lab/active` filters strictly on the real Lab-specific `LabExtension.experimentState === "active"` — LAB_UNIVERSE_v1.1.md §14's own literal lifecycle value — distinct from both Work's editorial `featured` and Research's universal `lifecycle`. The one real fixture (`experimentState: "abandoned"`) honestly does not match, so `/lab/active` is empty — while the overview's own "Active Assembly" highlight uses §11's *broader* "active or recently meaningful... experimental significance, not recency" criterion and *does* surface the same fixture, with its abandoned state shown plainly. Both are true and consistent simultaneously — a strict state filter and a broader editorial-significance zone, exactly as the Universe document itself distinguishes them.
2. **Grouping lens.** `/lab/prototypes` and `/lab/tools` map onto the real M3.1 content types `"prototype"`/`"tool"`. `/lab/studies` maps onto `"experiment"` — Lab's foundational record type (LAB_UNIVERSE_v1.1.md §1) and the one type the current fixture actually declares, so unlike Prototypes/Tools, Studies genuinely surfaces real content. "Studies" is this route's own editorial label for that type (matching `districtNavigation.ts`'s own pre-existing approved label), not a new taxonomy value.
3. **Comparison.** Lab is the only one of the three districts wired to consume `Comparison` (M4.10) at all — Work and Research have no real comparable-pair concept in their own Universe documents; Lab's own §13 ("Iteration as Spatial Structure") and §20 ("Earlier iteration" as an approved relationship label) make version/lineage comparison a defining district behavior. See "Comparison" below for what this package actually wires versus what current fixture data can exercise.
