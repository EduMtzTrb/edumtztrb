## Existing Home Audit

1. **What Home already had.** `src/app/page.tsx` was still the M4.9 prototype: `WorldThresholdShell` with heading literally "Home," one `ThresholdIndicator` (Work→Home arrival), one `ThresholdLink` to `/work`. No composition, no other district, no real content.
2. **What was scaffolding.** The entire visible content — a bare "Home" heading and a single "Enter Work" link — was placeholder, not Home content. `WorldThresholdShell` itself (M1.5), `AppShell`/`GlobalNav`/`WorldTrace` (M1.4/M3.7/M4.9), and the M4.9 threshold mechanism (`ThresholdLink`, `ThresholdIndicator`, `thresholdKeys.ts`, `familiarity.ts`) were real, tested, and unaffected by this gap.
3. **What M5.1 had to add.** Real orientation content, district discoverability for all six districts, and a spatial composition consuming M5.0's shared grammar — all still missing before this package.
4. **What must stay shared/systemic.** `WorldThresholdShell`, `AppShell`, `GlobalNav`, `WorldTrace`, the M4.9 threshold contract, `DistrictField`, and every Design System token — none were forked or duplicated.
5. **What is Home-specific and allowed here.** The actual page content/copy, and one small new stylesheet (`src/styles/home.css`) for the six-item district-threshold list, which does not fit `DistrictField`'s two-role (primary/peripheral) shape — explicitly anticipated by M5.0's own boundary ("Home-specific additions are allowed").

`CanonicalOwnerDistrict` (`src/domain/records/CanonicalRecord.ts`) does not include `"home"` — confirmed unchanged. `DistrictShell` requires a `CanonicalOwnerDistrict`, so it is structurally inapplicable to Home; Home continues to use `WorldThresholdShell` directly at the page level, never a `layout.tsx` + `DistrictShell` pair like the six districts.

## Home Role

Home is implemented as the entrance to the platform, not a résumé homepage, portfolio grid, marketing hero, dashboard, card collection, generic "welcome" page, splash screen, or cinematic intro. The composition establishes, in order: identity (heading = the approved Central Brand Idea, not the word "Home"), orientation (the approved Positioning Statement), and district availability (six one-sentence thresholds) — nothing more is loaded on first encounter.

## Spatial Concept

Home does not use `DistrictShell` (structurally distinct, per the audit above) and is not forced through the six-district layout pattern. It consumes M5.0's shared compositional grammar directly: one `DistrictField` instance (`density="concentrated"`, matching DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §70's "strongest concentrated void, low default evidence density") for the orientation statement, and one Home-specific list (`.home-districts`, `src/styles/home.css`) for the six peer district thresholds — a shape `DistrictField`'s two-role (primary/peripheral) contract cannot honestly represent, since six peer entries are not a dominant/peripheral pair. `.home-districts` reuses the exact same `--grid-*`/`--gutter-*` tokens as `.composition` and `district-composition.css`, at the same three breakpoints — the same shared axis, not a fourth grid system.

## First Encounter

The first viewport carries exactly three things: the signature (h1, Display voice, the Central Brand Idea), one orientation sentence (the approved Positioning Statement), and the six district thresholds with one clause each. No project lists, publications, catalogues, CV sections, archives, or filters are loaded — those remain district/utility territory. This satisfies the Design System's identity function ("resolved at first encounter, while retaining visible capacity for relation, depth, and further discovery") — the district thresholds are the visible "capacity for... discovery."

## District Presence

All six permanent districts (Work, Research, Lab, Art, Products, About) are discoverable via their exact approved canonical routes (`/work`, `/research`, `/lab`, `/art`, `/products`, `/about`) — no invented discipline is presented as a seventh top-level destination. Presence is expressed as a `<dl>` of name→one-sentence-framing pairs (PLATFORM_SPECIFICATION.md's own canonical operational distinctions, quoted verbatim), not a duplicate of `GlobalNav`'s "Primary" navigation landmark (a `<section aria-label="Districts">` region, not a second `<nav>`) — real content with real framing, not mechanical navigation duplication. No district receives special visual treatment (color, size, position) that would imply a permanent hierarchy, per HOME_UNIVERSE.md §10 ("No single threshold may dominate the composition").

## Authorship

Authorship is expressed through the approved Central Brand Idea (heading) and Positioning Statement (orientation paragraph) only — no full biography, CV content, or project list. `PLATFORM_VISION.md`'s Approved Authorship Model requires the platform to eventually operate under the person's real name; no governing document supplies that name, so none is invented here (see "Content Boundary" and "Deferred").

## District Entry

**Decision, made explicitly rather than silently:** the M4.9 Home↔Work threshold mechanism (`ThresholdLink`, `ThresholdIndicator`, `WorldTrace`, `thresholdKeys.ts`) is reused completely unchanged for Work — the one district with reciprocal arrival-side wiring — and is **not** generalized to the other five districts in this package.

Reasoning: `WorldTrace`'s own doc comment explicitly scopes itself to "Home <-> Work ONLY... not a mechanism for choreographing arbitrary route changes," and its CSS (`threshold.css`) hardcodes real positional percentages for exactly two trace positions — generalizing to six would require inventing five more relative positions along a shared trace axis, a genuine new spatial-design decision this package has no dedicated authority to make unilaterally, and `M4.9_HOME_THRESHOLD_PROTOTYPE.md` itself explicitly reserves that decision ("Milestone 5 decides the real, broader strategy once every district genuinely needs it"). The M5.1 IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md "Verify" bar is "all districts reachable," not "all districts have full first/repeated-entry choreography" — reachability is satisfied honestly by real canonical-route links. Using `ThresholdLink` (which marks a departure key) for a district with no reciprocal arrival-side consumer would be inert complexity presented as more than it is — the opposite of "no fabricated Home passage."

The other five links use `ActionLink` (`kind="route"`, M2.5) — the approved route-link control, giving them the same `.link`/`.type-label` visual treatment as Work's `ThresholdLink` (which now accepts an additive `className` prop for exactly this reason) without a fabricated arrival-side indicator.
