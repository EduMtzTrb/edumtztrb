## About vs Research

About is interpretive, not evidentiary (ABOUT_UNIVERSE_v1.1.md §1: "About does not repeat those districts. It identifies the person and practice that connect them... Its role is interpretive, not evidentiary"). Research is inquiry-oriented — its canonical record's primary evidence is a live Question, deliberately kept at ordinary weight so it never reads as resolved (see `docs/implementation/M5.3_RESEARCH.md` "Research vs Work"). About has no "Question" at all — its own content model (§28) is biographical fact (organization, role, period, institution, qualification), not an open inquiry. Structurally, About also has no curated-vs-comprehensive split the way Research does (`/research/current` vs `/research/archive`) — About is one continuous record presented as fixed structural sections (Position/Biography/Timeline/Practice/Values/Now/Contact, §7), never an archive of many records. This package's own tests assert both distinctions directly (no `/about/archive`, no `/about/selected`).
