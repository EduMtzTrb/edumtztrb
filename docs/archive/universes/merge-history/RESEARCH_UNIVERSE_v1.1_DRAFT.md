## 1. Purpose

**Research develops understanding.**

Research makes the intellectual structure of the practice visible: the questions being pursued, the evidence being assembled, the methods being refined, the positions still open. It is not a publication list, an academic résumé, or a document repository — it is where knowledge can be entered, followed, and revisited.

> Work demonstrates capability. Research develops understanding. Lab tests and transforms.

Research is the intellectual middle ground between observation and application.

---

## 2. Core Identity

An **architectural observatory and living archive**. The observatory looks outward — questions, tensions, unknowns. The archive looks inward — sources, methods, arguments, accumulated knowledge. Together: analytical, contemplative, unfinished by design, cumulative.

> Research is not displayed as a shelf of finished documents. It is experienced as an architecture of connected questions.

A visitor should leave understanding that research is part of the same practice as architecture, computation, software, AI, and art; that questions and methods matter as much as finished publications; that uncertainty is organized, not concealed; and that the district can grow for years without becoming an incoherent archive.

---

## 3. Research, Work, and Lab

Distinction by **purpose and state**, never by discipline or tool.

- **Work** is outcome-oriented: completed projects, delivered systems, verified capabilities. *What was made, delivered, or achieved?*
- **Research** is inquiry-oriented: questions, evidence, interpretation, evolving positions. *What is being investigated, and how is understanding changing?*
- **Lab** tests: experiments, prototypes, simulations, failures, technical evidence.

**Ownership rule:** when the value is the delivered outcome, Work owns it. When the value is the question, method, or intellectual position, Research owns it. When the value is the act of testing, Lab owns it. The same body of activity may have entries in more than one district — Research explaining the reasoning behind a method, Work showing its professional application — connected, never duplicated.

The Research–Lab relationship is explicitly **cyclical**, not a one-way pipeline:

```text
Research question → Lab experiment → Observed result → Revised research question
```

A Lab result can send the visitor back to Research just as often as a Research question sends them into Lab.

---

## 4. Knowledge as Architecture

The platform's architectural language becomes a model of knowledge itself, not just its container:

**Territories** (domains of inquiry) · **Axes** (recurring lines of thought) · **Foundations** (sources, precedents) · **Rooms** (focused questions) · **Thresholds** (transitions between subjects) · **Openings** (unresolved questions) · **Voids** (meaningful absences) · **Extensions** (new inquiry emerging from existing work).

The felt result: a visitor doesn't encounter isolated publications, but a field with orientation — pathways between ideas, areas of concentration, areas deliberately left open. Architecture here means proportion, sequence, hierarchy, adjacency — never a literal virtual library or decorative 3D interior.

---

## 5. Atmosphere

A quiet, expansive room whose limits aren't immediately visible — closer to a reading room after hours or an unfinished drawing table than a database. Calm rather than dramatic, curious rather than authoritative, rigorous without being institutionally academic.

**Temporal quality:** Research should feel slower than Work and more stable than Lab — supporting sustained attention and return, encouraging lingering without forcing delay.

---

## 6. Experience Narrative

Arrival → Orientation → Exploration → Focus → Connection → Return.

The visitor crosses a quiet threshold into an intellectual landscape (not a feed or a publication grid), sees territories positioned in relation to one another, approaches one to reveal its internal structure (questions, lines, sources, connected work), and follows a stable hierarchy down to a specific question or artifact:

```text
Research Universe → Territory → Research Line → Question / Artifact
```

At relevant moments the visitor sees that a question generated a professional application in Work, an experiment in Lab, or a method in Products — shown as **continuations of thought**, never as promotional recommendations. Returning to the overview restores the previous orientation; the district supports memory of place.

---

## 7. Homepage

Six zones, each with one job:

- **Threshold** — identity statement, minimal text, room to breathe.
- **Research Field** — a navigable field of territories; every visible connection must represent a real relationship, never a decorative node network.
- **Active Edge** — research lines currently receiving attention. Not a news feed — the active edge of the knowledge structure.
- **Archive Entrance** — systematic browsing: index, chronology, status, method, search.
- **Cross-Universe Passages** — which research informed completed work, which questions are being tested, which experiments changed the research.
- **Wider Universe** — clear paths back to Home, Work, Lab.

---

## 8. Navigation

Two equal modes: **spatial** (discovery, conceptual orientation, relationships) and **index** (direct lookup, filtering, accessibility, large archives). Neither is secondary — both display the same structured knowledge in different forms.

Deep pages show a stable hierarchy: `Research / Territory / Research Line / Question or Artifact`. Search behaves as a research instrument: every result explains what matched, where it belongs, its current state, and what it connects to.

---

## 9. Movement Between Research, Work, and Lab

Each direction of travel carries one sentence of meaning:

| Passage | Communicates |
|---|---|
| Research → Work | *This idea became consequential in practice.* |
| Research → Lab | *This question is being tested here.* |
| Lab → Research | *This result changed the inquiry.* |
| Work → Research | *This project belongs to a wider line of thought.* |

Passages appear only when a genuine relationship exists, name that relationship explicitly, preserve the visitor's context, and never duplicate the destination's content. Approved relationship labels: `Informed by`, `Applied in`, `Tested through`, `Generated by`, `Extended in`, `Reframed by`, `Developed into`.

---

## 10. Organization Model

```text
Research Universe
└── Territory
    └── Research Line
        ├── Question
        └── Artifact
```

Inline form: `Research Universe → Territory → Research Line → Question / Artifact`

- **Territory** — a broad, durable area of inquiry, capable of holding several research lines over years.
- **Research Line** — a sustained investigation within one or more territories.
- **Question** — a specific unresolved or recurring inquiry; may connect multiple lines.
- **Artifact** — a concrete output: publication, essay, diagram, dataset, method, note collection, research prototype.
- **Source** and **Method** operate across the hierarchy rather than owning a level of it.

**Questions and artifacts are sibling entities within a Research Line.** An artifact may address, document, challenge, or connect to one or more questions — it is not structurally required to be the child of exactly one question.

**Lifecycle:** Seed → Active → Paused → Developed → Published → Archived. These describe research activity, not project-approval status.

---

## 11. Editorial Language

The district's voice governs how the platform talks about evidence, and it's worth stating precisely because it's meant to influence writing elsewhere on the platform too.

**Questions before claims.** A visitor should understand what's being asked before encountering outputs or conclusions.

**Precision without academic performance.** No inflated claims, no unexplained jargon, no artificial certainty, no generic declarations of innovation.

**The state of knowledge stays visible.** Writing distinguishes question, observation, hypothesis, method, evidence, interpretation, and provisional versus established conclusion. The interface must never make a hypothesis look like a verified result.

**Preferred sequence:** Question → Context → Position → Method → Evidence → Development → Connections → Open ends. Not every page needs every layer; the logic should hold regardless.

This editorial rigor should influence the wider platform's honesty about evidence and uncertainty — without making every district sound academic. Each keeps its own voice.

---

## 12. Layout Principles

Structured working surfaces, not publication templates: strong underlying grid, large negative space, precise annotations, disciplined density. On wide screens the research field may dominate the viewport; on small screens, territories become a deliberate vertical sequence with hierarchy replacing spatial distance — no essential content depends on horizontal exploration.

An artifact should never appear as an isolated PDF or detached media object — the surrounding interface always indicates what question it belongs to and what preceded and followed it.

---

## 13. Interaction Philosophy

Interaction exists to explain hierarchy, relation, change, and state — not to make the district feel technological. At rest, only essential orientation is visible; focus reveals description, status, and connections progressively.

Motion behaves like changing scale within an architectural drawing — controlled zoom, planar translation, layer separation — never ornamental particles, disorienting rotation, or decorative network animation. Hover may enrich desktop use; no essential content depends on it.

---

## 14. Relationship with Other Universes

Research preserves intellectual and methodological foundations; **Work** owns the professional outcome; **Lab** owns the experiment; **Art** may translate research into perceptual or speculative form without Research absorbing the artwork into an academic structure; **Products** may operationalize research into a publication or tool while Research preserves its intellectual lineage; **About** summarizes research interests and trajectory while Research holds the detailed structure; **Home** introduces Research as one district while Research preserves the shared environmental laws.

---

## 15. Scalability

A small initial state — a few territories and lines — must feel intentional, not thin; empty space communicates capacity, not gaps. Growth adds territories, deeper hierarchies, institutional affiliations, and new artifact types. Long-term, the district may support citation relationships, public bibliographies, and thematic collections — future capacities, not present commitments.

**What stays stable regardless of scale:** the Universe → Territory → Research Line → Question / Artifact hierarchy, the spatial/index navigation split, territories as durable organizers, explicit relationships between entities, and clear ownership across universes.

---

## 16. Accessibility

Fully usable without atmospheric motion or spatial visualization: every spatial view has a structured textual equivalent, keyboard focus is visible, reduced motion preserves hierarchy, and cross-universe relationships remain understandable to assistive technologies. This is part of the dual-mode concept, not a fallback version.

---

## 17. Anti-Patterns

A generic academic profile. A résumé section. A chronological blog. A publication grid. A PDF repository. A decorative knowledge graph. A duplicate of Work or Lab. Complexity presented as evidence of rigor. Preliminary material presented with the authority of completed research.

---

## 18. Document Boundaries

This document owns Research's identity, atmosphere, experience narrative, homepage concept, navigation logic, organization model, cross-universe movement, and editorial language. It does not own the global sitemap, final typography, shared component specifications, or individual research content, thesis arguments, or paper content. Coordination remains required with `PLATFORM_VISION.md`, `INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `HOME_UNIVERSE.md`, `WORK_UNIVERSE.md`, and `LAB_UNIVERSE.md`.

## 19. Approved Experience Principles

Reproduced verbatim from `RESEARCH_UNIVERSE_v1.0.md` §20, in full, for traceability. No item added, removed, or reworded.

1. **Research is experienced as connected inquiry, not accumulated documents.**
2. **Knowledge organization behaves architecturally through hierarchy, adjacency, depth, and threshold.**
3. **Questions appear before claims.**
4. **Uncertainty remains visible, organized, and correctly labeled.**
5. **Spatial exploration and precise indexing are equally valid.**
6. **Every visible relationship represents a real relationship.**
7. **Research, Work, and Lab remain distinct through purpose and ownership.**
8. **Cross-universe passages explain how thought becomes testing or application.**
9. **The homepage remains curated; the archive provides completeness.**
10. **Growth adds content and relationships without changing the district's core model.**
11. **Atmosphere supports attention but never obstructs access.**
12. **The editorial voice remains rigorous, restrained, and honest about the state of knowledge.**

## 20. Approval Decision

Reproduced from `RESEARCH_UNIVERSE_v1.0.md` §22, unchanged.

The Research Universe is approved as: *a quiet architectural observatory and living archive where knowledge is experienced as a structured field of connected questions, methods, evidence, and evolving positions.*

Formally distinguished from Work (resolved application) and Lab (experimental testing). Its homepage is a dual system — atmospheric field and precise index. Its cross-universe movement is a system of explicit intellectual passages. Its editorial role can influence the platform's honesty about evidence without making every district sound academic.

---

## 21. Final Definition

Research does not present knowledge as a finished collection. It presents knowledge as architecture — territories, structures, passages, layers, and open spaces. Visitors enter through an atmospheric map of inquiry, move into focused research lines, inspect questions and artifacts, and follow explicit passages toward Work or Lab whenever an idea becomes applied or tested.

**The Research Universe is where the platform makes its thinking visible.**
