# Personal Platform — About Universe (Review Draft)

**Reviews:** `ABOUT_UNIVERSE_v1.0.md`
**Governing concept preserved:** The Archive House
**Closing statement in v1.0:** a governing quote, not the "District is where the platform ___" tagline used by Home, Work, Lab, and Art — see note below.

---

## Reviewer's Note

About is the document most exposed to becoming generic — "personal brand" writing is the genre most prone to marketing language, and this document mostly avoids it. §6 ("Communicating Personal Authorship") and its prohibited-tone list are the strongest part of the whole seven-document set at naming exactly what to avoid: "generic declarations of passion," "personal-brand slogans unsupported by evidence." That list should be read by whoever eventually writes the actual biography copy, not just filed as governance.

Three edits:

1. **§28 (Content Model)** listed thirteen record types field-by-field across sixty-plus lines. Necessary for implementation, tedious as prose. Reformatted as a compact reference table — nothing removed, just no longer read top to bottom as narrative.
2. **Terminology fix, cross-document.** This document's four/five-part formula (§1) says Lab "exposes transformation." Lab's own document says, consistently, "tests and transforms." **Corrected here to match** — same fix applied in the Art review, for the same reason: Lab is the authoritative source for its own verb.
3. **Missing closing tagline** — About ends on a governing quote rather than the bold "*District* is where the platform ___" pattern used elsewhere. Proposed: *"About is where the platform becomes a person."* **Proposed addition, not an approved decision** — flagged in the Review Report alongside the same gap in Research and Products.

One boundary question is **not resolved here** and is carried to the Review Report as a conflict: `PRODUCTS_UNIVERSE.md` §24 assigns "seller identity, rights-holder information, commercial disclosures" to "About, Legal, and Business Structure," but this document's own §27 lists "transaction information" under what About explicitly does not own, and nowhere claims commercial or legal disclosure content. About's content model should not be silently expanded to cover this gap — it needs an explicit decision about whether that information belongs in About, in Products' Store Information pages, or in a small dedicated legal/business layer neither document currently owns.

Nothing here changes the About/Work boundary (§11), the biography depth system, or the personal-information governance in §19 — the parts of this document doing the real protective work.

---

## 1. Governing Principle

> **About reveals authorship, continuity, and intent.**

The platform already contains its evidence:

```text
Work demonstrates capability.
Research develops understanding.
Lab tests and transforms.
Art concentrates perception.
Products operationalizes value.
```

About does not repeat those districts. It identifies the person and practice that connect them. Its role is interpretive, not evidentiary — explaining how the different bodies of work belong to one evolving design practice, what shaped it, and what direction it's taking.

---

## 2. Role Within the Platform

About is where a visitor understands who is responsible for the platform, how the practice developed, why multiple disciplines coexist, and what forms of collaboration are relevant now.

**About is not where credibility begins.** Credibility should already be visible throughout the platform. About gives that credibility authorship, chronology, and meaning — answering the one question the other districts can't answer on their own: *what kind of person and practice could have produced this complete body of work?*

---

## 3. Core Experience

Home introduces the world. The other districts reveal what exists within it. **About reveals its author.**

The visitor should not feel they've left the architectural world to read a conventional profile page — they should feel they've entered its most inhabited district, closer to an archive, studio, or study than a corporate bio page. The metaphor is conceptual — accumulated time, selected evidence, present activity — never a literal domestic interior.

---

## 4. Why About Is Developed Last

About interprets the complete practice — it cannot define that practice before Work, Research, Lab, Art, and Products establish their own boundaries. Developed last, it can describe the actual continuity between them without forcing a premature personal narrative onto the rest of the platform. **The work remains primary; authorship becomes legible after the evidence, not instead of it.**

---

## 5. Primary Audiences and the Dual-Speed Principle

Two compatible reading modes, not two separate pages:

- **Professional decision-makers** — employers, clients, collaborators, institutions — need rapid clarity: position, disciplinary range, relevant experience, availability, contact. They should not need the full personal narrative to evaluate relevance.
- **Deeper explorers** — visitors who want to understand how the practice evolved and what recurs across disciplines. The district should reward this without requiring it.

**Immediate reading** (a professional summary, understood quickly) and **extended reading** (biography, timeline, values, unfolding progressively) must coexist. Neither should obstruct the other.

*(Note: `PLATFORM_VISION.md` §7 leaves the priority order among audiences an explicit open decision at the Vision level. This document's dual-speed principle is a reasonable interpretation but does not itself resolve that upstream openness — see the Review Report.)*

---

## 6. Communicating Personal Authorship

Authorship should emerge through clarity and selection, never self-promotion — a clear first-person position, recurring questions across disciplines, formative transitions, and values connected to actual methods. **The author should appear responsible for the work, not larger than it.**

**Tone:** direct, reflective, factual, precise — modest without hesitancy, confident without promotion, personal without confession.

**Avoid, specifically:** exaggerated claims, personal mythology, generic declarations of passion, inflated multidisciplinary labels, motivational language, personal-brand slogans unsupported by evidence, autobiographical detail included only to manufacture intimacy.

This is the platform's clearest statement of what "personal but not autobiographical" actually means in practice, and it should govern tone decisions in every other district too, not just About's own copy.

---

## 7. Information Architecture

```text
ABOUT
├── Position
├── Biography
├── Timeline
├── Practice (Experience · Education · Methods · Languages · Credentials)
├── Values
├── Now
└── Contact
```

Conceptual, not a prescription of final routes — these layers may live as one continuous district or as linked internal views.

---

## 8. Entrance: Position

The first view establishes presence and relevance — not the complete biography. It states name, concise professional position, operating context, primary disciplinary territory, and a short statement connecting the practice — plus a clear path toward professional facts and a clear path toward deeper exploration.

**The position statement connects** architecture, computational design, software, AI, research, art, and product development as related modes of one practice. It does not present them as a list of unrelated skills.

**A portrait is permitted, not mandatory** — when used, it identifies rather than displays. A large promotional headshot must not dominate the entrance.

---

## 9. Biography System

Three depths, each with a distinct use, not three drafts of the same text:

- **Micro** (one or two sentences) — entrance, metadata, compact contexts.
- **Short** (one paragraph) — professional evaluation, speaking introductions.
- **Extended** — a structured narrative, available to visitors seeking it, never required reading.

**The extended biography follows six movements:** Origin (the initial relationship with architecture, design, or making) → Formation (educational and professional foundations) → Expansion (the movement into computation, software, AI, research, art) → Convergence (the point these fields became one practice rather than separate interests) → Present → Direction.

**Must:** use concrete transitions, distinguish past from present, remain consistent with evidence shown elsewhere in the platform, stay updateable without a full rewrite.
**Must not:** reproduce a cover letter, narrate every life event, manufacture a predetermined heroic arc, or overstate the coherence of earlier stages before that coherence actually existed.

---

## 10. Timeline

Visualizes continuity, not a résumé — how disciplines, places, and questions developed in parallel and gradually converged.

**Multi-track**, with tracks for education, professional practice, research, computational development, art, products, and major conceptual shifts — allowed to overlap, branch, pause, or converge, making the trajectory legible as a developing system rather than a sequence of isolated jobs.

**Event hierarchy:** Pivotal (changed the practice's direction) → Developmental (deepened a competence) → Contextual (chronology, location, institutional context). On mobile, the timeline becomes a clear chronological sequence, not a compressed multi-track diagram.

---

## 11. Professional Experience

**Governing distinction:**

> About explains the professional trajectory. Work demonstrates professional capability through projects.

Experience belongs in About when it describes roles, organizations, and methods used across multiple projects. It belongs in Work when it describes one specific project, outcome, or process. Each About entry should summarize a role, not reproduce every duty — and should support both **narrative mode** (how the role shaped the evolving practice) and **reference mode** (facts for rapid professional evaluation).

---

## 12. Education

Presented as formation, not prestige display — institution, qualification, field, and the entry's relationship to current practice. Not an archive of every course or grade; detailed academic outputs belong in Research, Work, or Lab according to their nature.

---

## 13. Values

Values explain how decisions are made, not abstract branding claims. Each requires four parts: **the principle, its interpretation, its consequence in practice, and its tension or limitation.** A commitment to openness, for instance, should also explain when decisions must become resolved.

This four-part structure — principle, interpretation, consequence, tension — is what prevents values from reading as slogans, and is the section's most important rule. Possible territories: rigor, adaptability, care, precision, experimentation, long-term thinking — the final set selected from demonstrated patterns, not an aspirational brand persona.

---

## 14. Methods

Methods are durable ways of working; tools are temporary instruments. Possible territories: spatial reasoning, systems thinking, computational modeling, prototyping, iteration, editorial judgment, reflective practice. Tools may be named where professionally useful but stay secondary to the methods they enable.

**Avoid:** skill percentages, decorative software-logo walls, exhaustive tool inventories, unqualified claims of mastery.

---

## 15. Languages, Location, and Availability

Visible, current, and easy to update. Languages carry honest proficiency descriptions. Location communicates operating context without exposing a private address. **Availability is dynamic information** — available, selectively available, open to commissions, fully committed — and should never create false urgency or function as a sales device.

---

## 16. Collaborators, Clients, Employers, and Institutions

The platform must distinguish employment from collaboration, consultancy, and authorship — association with an organization never implies ownership of everything produced there. Clients may be named where permission exists and naming adds context; client logos should never become the primary proof of credibility. A structured People or Network layer emerges only once recurring collaborations become significant enough to need it beyond individual project credits.

---

## 17. CV, Résumé, and Credentials

The on-page summary must give enough structured information that **a visitor is never forced to download a document to understand the profile.** The CV is the comprehensive secondary reference; a résumé may offer a shorter, role-specific version. Expired or inactive credentials must not be presented as current. Outdated document versions should be archived or removed from public navigation, not left to accumulate.

---

## 18. Contact Experience

Contact should feel like opening a relevant channel, not entering a conversion funnel — direct, calm, low-friction. The visitor may identify a context (employment, commissions, research, press) but should not be forced through a complex qualification process.

**Required fields:** name, reply address, message. **Avoid:** mandatory phone number, marketing consent, budget interrogation before conversation, hidden direct contact information.

---

## 19. Appropriate Personal Information

The district's central discipline: personal enough to reveal authorship, disciplined enough to preserve privacy.

**Appropriate:** selected formative experiences, intellectual influences, methods of working, carefully chosen portraiture. **Conditional** (included only when clearly relevant): age, nationality, migration history, personal beliefs. **Excluded, always:** private address, personal phone number, financial information, legal identifiers, unfiltered personal archives, information about other people without consent.

**The governing test:** a personal detail belongs only when it materially helps the visitor understand authorship, continuity, method, value, context, or intent. **Curiosity alone is not sufficient.** This single sentence is the section's real rule; everything else in §19 is an application of it.

---

## 20. Visual and Spatial Identity — The Archive House

A quiet archive house within the shared architectural world: inhabited, intimate, reflective, temporally layered. Compared with other districts, About has a smaller perceived scale and greater proximity — conceptually a sequence of threshold, identity chamber, biography passage, timeline gallery, values room, current desk, and contact aperture.

About stays governed by the platform's shared typography, spatial logic, and navigation. Its distinct identity comes from intimacy, scale, and pacing — never from an unrelated graphic style.

---

## 21. Layout and Rhythm

Alternates between concise professional facts, open spatial pauses, and layered chronology — long biography text divided into meaningful movements rather than one continuous block. Images function as evidence, memory, or trace, never as mere illustration of the adjacent paragraph.

---

## 22. Navigation and Cross-District Relationships

Primary destinations: Position, Story, Timeline, Practice, Values, Now, Contact. Contextual links lead to canonical records in Work, Research, Lab, Art, and Products — About may explain *why* a relationship exists but never duplicates the destination's content.

| Relationship | Boundary |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Home introduces the world. About reveals its author. |
| **Work** | About explains the career trajectory. Work demonstrates resolved practice. |
| **Research** | About identifies recurring questions. Research contains the investigations. |
| **Lab** | About explains the experimental disposition. Lab contains the tests and transformations. |
| **Art** | About may provide personal context. Art remains autonomous and is never reduced to biography. |
| **Products** | About explains the maker or organizational direction. Products owns the offer and transaction layer. |

---

## 23. Evolution from Personal Practice to Studio or Company

```text
PERSON → PERSONAL PRACTICE → STUDIO → COMPANY
```

Each stage retains an explicit relationship to the original author — the transition must feel evolutionary, never a replacement of the platform. As more people contribute, the platform distinguishes founder authorship from collective and project authorship: **the original personal story is never rewritten to imply sole authorship of future collective work.**

---

## 24. Current State: Now

A maintained present-tense layer — current role, active focus, availability, emerging directions — clearly dated and easy to update without touching the stable biography or historical timeline. States: current, ongoing, emerging, paused, completed, archived.

---

## 25. Responsive Experience

Desktop may express parallel timeline tracks and layered relationships; mobile becomes a **direct, linear personal narrative** in a fixed priority order: identity → position → professional facts → biography → timeline → values and methods → current state → contact. Mobile must not simulate a compressed desktop archive.

---

## 26. Accessibility

Semantic biography structure, clear heading hierarchy, keyboard-accessible timeline controls, descriptive alternatives for images. **The timeline must always have a readable list-based representation.** CV and résumé documents must be accessible and clearly versioned.

---

## 27. Content Boundaries

**About owns:** authorship, professional position, biography, timeline, experience chronology, education, methods, values, languages, location, availability, credentials, collaborators at the practice level, organizational evolution, CV/résumé access, current state, contact.

**About does not own:** complete project case studies, research papers, experiment records, artwork presentation, product listings, **transaction information**, project-specific credits, private autobiography, unfiltered archives.

*(The bolded item is the exact boundary at issue in the Reviewer's Note above — Products' §24 assumes something adjacent to it belongs here, and this document says otherwise.)*

---

## 29. Visitor Outcome

The desired final impression is not *"this person does many different things."* It is:

> "This is one coherent practice expressed through multiple forms, with a clear history, authorship, method, and direction."

---

## 30. Anti-Patterns

Conventional résumé-page design. Self-promotional hero language. Personality replacing evidence. A giant portrait as primary content. Biography detached from the work. Generic values. Skills percentages. Borrowed prestige. Contact as a sales funnel. Redesigning the district the moment the practice becomes a studio.

---

## 31. Approved Decisions

Preserved from v1.0 without conceptual change.

1. About is an interpretive district, not another evidence repository.
2. Its governing distinction is: **About reveals authorship, continuity, and intent.**
3. The work remains primary; personal authorship contextualizes rather than dominates it.
4. The district supports both rapid professional evaluation and deeper personal exploration.
5. Biography exists at micro, short, and extended resolutions.
6. The timeline uses parallel tracks to reveal convergence across disciplines.
7. Experience explains roles and trajectory; project evidence remains in Work.
8. Education is presented as formation rather than prestige.
9. Values must be connected to demonstrated consequences and tensions.
10. Methods are emphasized over tool inventories.
11. Languages, location, and availability are visible, structured, and current.
12. Collaborators and institutions are represented through accurate relationship and attribution.
13. CV, résumé, and credentials are secondary professional references, not substitutes for the page.
14. Personal information is included only when it clarifies authorship, continuity, method, context, value, or intent.
15. Contact is an open channel rather than a conversion funnel.
16. The approved spatial identity is a quiet **Archive House** within the architectural world.
17. A maintained Now layer distinguishes the present from the stable biography.
18. The district is structured to evolve from person to personal practice, studio, and company without erasing original authorship.

---

## 32. Final Definition

About is the interpretive and human layer of the platform. It reveals the author without making the platform self-centered, organizing biography, experience, values, methods, and current activity into a coherent account of an evolving practice — letting professional decision-makers evaluate relevance quickly while rewarding those who want the deeper continuity.

> About reveals who is responsible, how the practice became coherent, what guides it, and where it intends to go.

**About is where the platform becomes a person.** *(Proposed tagline — see Reviewer's Note above.)*
