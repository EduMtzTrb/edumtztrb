# ABOUT_UNIVERSE.md

## Personal Platform — About Universe

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**District:** About  
**Document role:** Governing design and content framework  
**Scope:** Authorship, biography, continuity, professional context, values, credentials, availability, contact, and future organizational evolution  
**Excludes:** Final biography copy, private personal records, complete CV data, project case studies, implementation specifications, and final visual design tokens

---

# 1. Governing Principle

> **About reveals authorship, continuity, and intent.**

The platform already contains extensive evidence.

Work demonstrates capability.  
Research develops understanding.  
Lab exposes transformation.  
Art concentrates perception.  
Products operationalizes value.

About does not repeat those districts.

It identifies the person and practice that connect them.

Its role is interpretive rather than evidentiary. It explains how the different bodies of work belong to one evolving design practice, what shaped that practice, what principles guide it, and what direction it is taking.

---

# 2. Role Within the Platform

The About Universe is the district where the visitor understands:

- who is responsible for the platform
- how the practice developed
- why multiple disciplines coexist
- which values and methods recur across the work
- what experience supports the practice
- what the person is currently pursuing
- what forms of conversation or collaboration are relevant

About is therefore not the place where credibility begins.

Credibility should already be visible throughout the platform.

About gives that credibility authorship, chronology, and meaning.

The district should answer a question that the other universes cannot answer independently:

> What kind of person and practice could have produced this complete body of work?

---

# 3. Core Experience

## Encountering the author of the universe

The Home Universe introduces the world.

The other districts reveal what exists within it.

The About Universe reveals its author.

The visitor should not feel that they have left the architectural metaverse to read a conventional profile page. They should feel that they have entered its most inhabited district: a quieter environment where biography, experience, values, and intention appear as traces of a continuous practice.

The experience should resemble entering a restrained combination of:

- archive
- studio
- study
- observatory
- personal record
- working room

The architectural metaphor is not a literal domestic interior. It is a conceptual environment of accumulated time, selected evidence, and present activity.

---

# 4. Why About Is Developed Last

About interprets the complete practice.

It cannot define that practice before Work, Research, Lab, Art, and Products establish their own identities and boundaries.

Developed last, About can now describe the actual continuity between them without forcing a premature personal narrative onto the rest of the platform.

This sequencing protects the platform from becoming personality-led.

The work remains primary.

Authorship becomes legible after the visitor has encountered—or is able to encounter—the evidence.

# 5. Primary Audiences

About must support two distinct but compatible reading modes.

## 5.1 Professional decision-makers

This includes:

- employers
- clients
- commissioners
- collaborators
- institutions
- recruiters
- partners
- curators
- publishers
- educators
- investors or future commercial partners

These visitors need rapid clarity.

They should be able to identify:

- professional position
- disciplinary range
- relevant experience
- education
- location
- languages
- availability
- credentials
- contact route

They should not need to read the entire personal narrative to evaluate relevance.

## 5.2 Deeper explorers

Some visitors will want to understand:

- how the practice evolved
- what intellectual and creative concerns recur
- why disciplines overlap
- what values guide decisions
- how personal experiences influenced the work
- what future direction is emerging

The district should reward this deeper exploration without requiring it.

## 5.3 Dual-speed principle

About must function at two speeds:

### Immediate reading

A professional summary can be understood quickly.

### Extended reading

A fuller personal and disciplinary trajectory unfolds through biography, timeline, values, methods, and selected personal traces.

Neither mode should obstruct the other.

---

# 6. Communicating Personal Authorship

Personal authorship should be communicated through clarity, selection, and continuity rather than self-promotion.

The district should avoid presenting the author as the central spectacle of the platform.

Instead, authorship should emerge through:

- a clear first-person position
- a concise explanation of the practice
- recurring questions across disciplines
- formative transitions
- stated values connected to actual methods
- selected experience
- current areas of attention
- visible relationships to the work elsewhere

The author should appear responsible for the work, not larger than it.

## 6.1 Tone

The tone should be:

- direct
- reflective
- factual
- precise
- modest without becoming hesitant
- confident without becoming promotional
- personal without becoming confessional

## 6.2 Prohibited tone

Avoid:

- exaggerated claims
- personal mythology
- generic declarations of passion
- inflated multidisciplinary labels
- motivational language
- forced informality
- personal-brand slogans unsupported by evidence
- autobiographical detail included only to create intimacy

# 7. Information Architecture

The About Universe is organized into seven primary layers.

```text
ABOUT
│
├── Position
├── Biography
├── Timeline
├── Practice
│   ├── Experience
│   ├── Education
│   ├── Methods
│   ├── Languages
│   └── Credentials
├── Values
├── Now
└── Contact
```

These layers may exist within one continuous district or as linked internal views.

The structure is conceptual and does not prescribe final routes.

# 8. The Entrance: Position

The first view must provide immediate orientation.

It should communicate:

- name
- concise professional position
- location or operating context
- primary disciplinary territory
- a short statement connecting the practice
- current availability or status when relevant
- a clear path toward professional facts
- a clear path toward deeper exploration

The opening should not contain the complete biography.

Its purpose is to establish presence and relevance.

## 8.1 Position statement

The position statement should explain the practice without reducing it to one job title.

It should connect architecture, computational design, software, AI, research, art, and product development as related modes of design practice.

It should not present them as a list of unrelated skills.

## 8.2 Portrait

A portrait is permitted but not mandatory.

When used, it should function as identification rather than self-display.

Acceptable approaches include:

- restrained photographic portrait
- environmental portrait
- partial or indirect portrait
- portrait integrated with a working context
- quiet moving image
- abstract personal trace supported by clear identification elsewhere

A large promotional headshot should not dominate the entrance.

---

# 9. Biography System

Biography should exist at several levels of depth.

## 9.1 Micro biography

One or two sentences.

Used for:

- About entrance
- metadata
- external introductions
- compact profile contexts

## 9.2 Short biography

One concise paragraph.

Used for:

- professional evaluation
- speaking introductions
- collaborations
- press summaries
- institutional references

## 9.3 Extended biography

A structured narrative that explains the evolution of the practice.

It should be available to visitors seeking deeper context but should not be required reading.

## 9.4 Narrative structure

The extended biography should follow six movements.

### Origin

The initial relationship with architecture, design, systems, space, making, or representation.

### Formation

The educational, geographic, technical, and professional foundations of the practice.

### Expansion

The movement from architecture into computational design, software, AI, research, art, or product development.

### Convergence

The point at which these fields became understood as one practice rather than separate interests.

### Present

The current professional, creative, and research position.

### Direction

The questions, ambitions, or organizational possibilities shaping the next phase.

## 9.5 Biography rules

The biography must:

- use concrete transitions
- explain causes and relationships
- distinguish past and present
- acknowledge evolution
- remain consistent with evidence elsewhere
- be updateable without rewriting the entire district

It must not:

- reproduce a cover letter
- narrate every life event
- duplicate project descriptions
- manufacture a predetermined heroic arc
- overstate the coherence of earlier stages
- reveal personal information without a clear role

# 10. Timeline

## 10.1 Purpose

The timeline visualizes continuity.

It should reveal how disciplines, places, experiences, roles, and questions developed in parallel and gradually converged.

It is not merely a chronological résumé.

## 10.2 Multi-track structure

The timeline may include parallel tracks for:

- education
- professional practice
- independent practice
- research
- computational development
- art
- products
- geographic transitions
- major conceptual shifts

Tracks may overlap, branch, pause, or converge.

This makes the trajectory legible as a developing system rather than a sequence of isolated jobs.

## 10.3 Event hierarchy

Timeline events have three levels.

### Pivotal

Events that changed the direction of the practice.

### Developmental

Periods in which an area of competence or inquiry deepened.

### Contextual

Supporting facts that establish chronology, location, or institutional context.

## 10.4 Timeline behavior

The visitor should first understand the overall trajectory and then reveal detail.

The timeline should support:

- overview
- filtering by track
- selective expansion
- relationships between events
- contextual links to other universes
- a fully readable non-interactive version

On mobile, the timeline becomes a clear chronological sequence rather than a compressed multi-track diagram.

# 11. Professional Experience

Experience should establish professional credibility without duplicating Work.

## 11.1 Experience belongs in About when it describes:

- roles
- organizations
- responsibilities
- professional environments
- duration
- location
- scale of contribution
- methods used across multiple projects
- types of collaboration
- organizational development

## 11.2 Experience belongs in Work when it describes:

- a specific project
- a specific design problem
- a specific outcome
- visual project evidence
- process and resolution
- project-specific responsibilities
- project-specific impact

## 11.3 Governing distinction

> About explains the professional trajectory.  
> Work demonstrates professional capability through projects.

## 11.4 Experience entry structure

Each professional entry may include:

- organization
- role
- period
- location
- context
- responsibilities
- contribution
- methods
- areas of learning
- related Work records

The entry should summarize the role rather than reproduce every duty.

## 11.5 Reading modes

Professional experience must support:

### Narrative mode

How the role shaped the evolving practice.

### Reference mode

Clear facts for rapid professional evaluation.

---

# 12. Education

Education should be presented as formation rather than prestige display.

Each entry may include:

- institution
- qualification
- field
- period
- location
- relevant concentration
- relationship to the current practice
- selected research or thesis connection when appropriate

Education should explain the foundations of the multidisciplinary practice.

It should not become an archive of every course, grade, or academic activity.

Detailed academic outputs belong in Research, Work, or Lab according to their nature.

---

# 13. Values

Values explain how decisions are made.

They should not function as abstract branding claims.

Each value must include:

1. the principle
2. its interpretation
3. its consequence in practice
4. its tension or limitation

For example, a commitment to openness should also explain when decisions must become resolved.

This structure prevents values from becoming slogans.

## 13.1 Appropriate value territories

The framework may support values such as:

- rigor
- openness
- accessibility
- curiosity
- coherence
- adaptability
- care
- precision
- responsibility
- independence
- experimentation
- long-term thinking

The final set must be selected from demonstrated patterns in the practice.

Values should describe existing commitments, not an aspirational brand persona.

---

# 14. Methods

Methods explain how the person works across different disciplines.

They should connect the universes without becoming a software list.

Possible method territories include:

- spatial reasoning
- systems thinking
- drawing and representation
- research and interpretation
- computational modeling
- prototyping
- iteration
- technical coordination
- testing
- editorial judgment
- visual communication
- product thinking
- reflective practice

## 14.1 Methods versus tools

Methods are durable ways of working.

Tools are temporary instruments.

Tools may be named where professionally useful, but they should remain secondary to the capabilities and methods they enable.

Avoid:

- skill percentages
- decorative software-logo walls
- exhaustive tool inventories
- unqualified claims of mastery

---

# 15. Languages, Location, and Availability

These facts should be visible, current, and easy to update.

## 15.1 Languages

Languages should be presented with honest proficiency descriptions.

They may also indicate relevant professional use, such as:

- working language
- native language
- professional fluency
- conversational ability

Language information belongs in the professional summary and CV layer, not hidden within biography.

## 15.2 Location

Location should communicate the relevant operating context without exposing unnecessary personal detail.

Appropriate information includes:

- city
- country
- region of practice
- remote or international availability
- timezone where professionally useful
- relocation openness if relevant

A private residential address should never be displayed.

## 15.3 Availability

Availability should be treated as dynamic information.

Possible states include:

- available
- selectively available
- open to collaboration
- open to commissions
- open to employment
- unavailable
- fully committed
- future availability
- not publicly stated

Availability may be qualified by opportunity type.

It should never create false urgency or function as a sales device.

---

# 16. Collaborators, Clients, Employers, and Institutions

Other people and organizations must be represented accurately and proportionately.

## 16.1 Employers

Employers should appear within professional chronology and role context.

The platform must distinguish:

- employment
- collaboration
- consultancy
- authorship
- contribution
- leadership
- assistance

Association with an organization must not imply ownership of all work produced there.

## 16.2 Clients

Clients may be named where:

- permission exists
- the relationship is not confidential
- naming adds useful context
- attribution is accurate

Client logos should not become the primary proof of credibility.

## 16.3 Collaborators

Collaborators should be credited where their contribution is relevant.

The About Universe may include:

- recurring collaborators
- collective memberships
- co-founders
- advisors
- institutional partners
- teams associated with the evolution of the practice

Detailed project credits remain with canonical project records.

## 16.4 Institutions

Institutions may appear in relation to:

- education
- employment
- research
- exhibitions
- funding
- publication
- teaching
- awards
- partnerships

Their presence should establish context, not borrowed prestige.

## 16.5 Future collaborator layer

As the platform grows, About may include a structured People or Network layer.

This should emerge only when recurring collaborations become significant enough to require representation beyond individual project credits.

---

# 17. CV, Résumé, and Credentials

The About Universe should integrate professional documents without becoming dependent on them.

## 17.1 On-page professional summary

The district must provide enough structured information for a visitor to understand:

- position
- experience
- education
- methods
- languages
- location
- selected credentials
- current status

A visitor should not be forced to download a document to understand the profile.

## 17.2 CV

The CV is the comprehensive professional record.

It may include:

- full chronology
- education
- employment
- selected projects
- publications
- exhibitions
- awards
- teaching
- credentials
- technical capabilities
- languages

It should be available as a secondary reference.

## 17.3 Résumé

A résumé may provide a shorter, role-specific professional summary.

Multiple résumé versions may exist for different professional contexts, but only clearly current versions should be public.

## 17.4 Credentials

Credentials may include:

- degrees
- certifications
- licenses
- memberships
- awards
- formal appointments
- verified training

Each credential should state:

- issuing body
- title
- date
- status or validity when relevant

Expired or inactive credentials must not be presented as current.

## 17.5 Document governance

Public professional documents should include:

- language
- intended context
- version date
- current or archived status
- accessible file format

Outdated versions should be archived or removed from public navigation.

# 18. Contact Experience

## 18.1 Principle

Contact should feel like opening a relevant channel, not entering a conversion funnel.

The experience must remain direct, calm, and low-friction.

## 18.2 Contact contexts

The visitor may contact regarding:

- employment
- professional collaboration
- architecture or design work
- research
- teaching
- speaking
- commissions
- art
- products
- licensing
- press
- institutional activity
- general inquiry

The visitor may identify a context, but should not be forced through a complex qualification process.

## 18.3 Essential structure

The contact area should provide:

- a concise invitation
- a primary direct contact method
- optional context selection
- minimal message fields
- relevant external professional channels
- current location or timezone where useful
- clear confirmation after submission

## 18.4 Contact fields

Recommended required fields:

- name
- email or reply address
- message

Optional:

- organization
- reason for contact
- relevant link
- preferred language

Avoid:

- mandatory phone number
- marketing consent
- excessive qualification
- budget interrogation before conversation
- decorative complexity
- hidden direct contact information

## 18.5 Contact routing

As the platform develops, inquiries may be routed by subject to:

- personal practice
- studio
- products
- press
- research
- employment
- support

The visitor should still experience one coherent contact system.

# 19. Appropriate Personal Information

The About Universe should be personal enough to reveal authorship but disciplined enough to preserve privacy.

## 19.1 Appropriate information

Appropriate personal content may include:

- selected formative experiences
- professional motivations
- intellectual influences
- places that shaped the practice
- languages
- broad geographic history
- methods of working
- selected personal archives
- current interests directly related to the practice
- carefully chosen portraiture

## 19.2 Conditional information

The following may be included only when clearly relevant:

- age
- nationality
- personal history
- family influence
- health or disability
- migration history
- personal beliefs
- lifestyle
- personal routines

Such information should appear only when the author intentionally chooses it and when it clarifies the practice, context, or values.

## 19.3 Excluded information

The public district should not expose:

- private address
- personal phone number unless deliberately professional
- private family details
- sensitive identity data without explicit purpose
- financial information
- legal identifiers
- personal schedules
- unfiltered personal archives
- information about other people without consent

## 19.4 Governing test

A personal detail belongs only when it materially helps the visitor understand:

- authorship
- continuity
- method
- value
- context
- intent

Curiosity alone is not sufficient.

- threshold
- identity chamber
- biography passage
- timeline gallery
- experience archive
- values room
- current desk
- contact aperture

## 20.3 Visual characteristics

About may introduce:

- warmer light
- finer line weights
- archival labels
- measured intervals
- restrained annotations
- translucent layers
- personal documents
- sketches
- notebook fragments
- environmental portraits

About remains governed by the platform’s shared:

# 21. Layout and Rhythm

The district should alternate between:

- concise professional facts
- open spatial pauses
- narrow biographical passages
- layered chronology
- selected personal evidence
- isolated value statements
- current information
- direct contact

Long biography text should be divided into meaningful movements.

Images should function as:

- evidence
- memory
- context
- temporal marker
- trace
- atmosphere

They should not merely illustrate adjacent paragraphs.

The page should support scanning without flattening everything into identical cards.

# 22. Navigation and Cross-District Relationships

Primary internal destinations are:

- Position
- Story
- Timeline
- Practice
- Values
- Now
- Contact

Contextual links may connect to:

- Work
- Research
- Lab
- Art
- Products

These links should lead to canonical records.

About may explain why a relationship exists but should not duplicate the destination’s content.

## 22.1 Relationship to Home

Home introduces the world.  
About reveals its author.

## 22.2 Relationship to Work

About explains the career and capability trajectory.  
Work demonstrates resolved professional practice.

## 22.3 Relationship to Research

About identifies recurring questions and intellectual development.  
Research contains the investigations and knowledge records.

## 22.4 Relationship to Lab

About explains the experimental disposition.  
Lab contains unstable tests, iterations, and transformations.

## 22.5 Relationship to Art

About may provide personal context.  
Art remains autonomous and must not be reduced to biography.

## 22.6 Relationship to Products

About explains the maker, entrepreneurial, or organizational direction.  
Products owns the offer and transaction layer.

# 23. Evolution from Personal Practice to Studio or Company

The About Universe must support organizational growth without erasing personal authorship.

## 23.1 Personal practice stage

Initially, About is centered on one author.

It communicates:

- individual biography
- individual experience
- personal methods
- values
- current activity
- contact

## 23.2 Emerging studio stage

As recurring collaborators, shared processes, and independent commissions grow, the district may distinguish:

- Founder
- Practice
- Collaborators
- Network
- Studio direction

The personal biography remains visible, but the practice gains its own identity.

## 23.3 Company stage

If the platform evolves into a company, About may expand to include:

- organizational purpose
- company history
- leadership
- team
- operating principles
- capabilities
- locations
- partners
- governance
- careers
- press resources

## 23.4 Continuity principle

The transition must feel evolutionary, not like a replacement of the platform.

The structure should move from:

```text
PERSON
  ↓
PERSONAL PRACTICE
  ↓
STUDIO
  ↓
COMPANY
```

Each stage retains an explicit relationship to the original author and governing intent.

## 23.5 Authorship at scale

As more people contribute, the platform must distinguish:

- founder authorship
- collective authorship
- project authorship
- institutional ownership
- collaborator contribution

The original personal story should not be rewritten to imply sole authorship of future collective work.

# 26. Accessibility

About must prioritize retrieval and comprehension.

Requirements include:

- semantic biography structure
- clear heading hierarchy
- readable line lengths
- adequate contrast
- keyboard-accessible timeline controls
- reduced-motion support
- descriptive alternatives for images
- captions for archival material
- non-visual equivalents for spatial diagrams
- explicit form labels
- clear error and confirmation states
- no essential information communicated only through motion, color, or position

The timeline must always have a readable list-based representation.

CV and résumé documents must be accessible and clearly versioned.

# 29. Visitor Outcome

After leaving the About Universe, the visitor should understand:

1. who is responsible for the platform
2. what kind of practice is being developed
3. how architecture, computation, AI, research, art, and products belong together
4. how the practice evolved
5. what professional experience and education support it
6. which values and methods guide decisions
7. where the person is based and how they work
8. which languages they use
9. what they are currently pursuing
10. whether a relevant opportunity or collaboration is possible
11. how to make contact
12. how the personal practice could evolve into a studio or company

The desired final impression is not:

> “This person does many different things.”

It is:

> “This is one coherent practice expressed through multiple forms, with a clear history, authorship, method, and direction.”

# 31. Approved Decisions

The following decisions are approved in Version 1.0:

1. About is an interpretive district, not another evidence repository.
2. Its governing distinction is: **About reveals authorship, continuity, and intent.**
3. The work remains primary; personal authorship contextualizes rather than dominates it.
4. The district supports both rapid professional evaluation and deeper personal exploration.
5. Biography exists at micro, short, and extended resolutions.
6. The timeline uses parallel tracks to reveal convergence across disciplines.
7. Experience explains roles and trajectory; project evidence remains in Work.
8. Education is presented as formation rather than prestige.
9. Values must be connected to demonstrated consequences and tensions.
10. Methods are emphasized over tool inventories.
11. Languages, location, and availability are visible, structured, and current.
12. Collaborators and institutions are represented through accurate relationship and attribution.
13. CV, résumé, and credentials are secondary professional references, not substitutes for the page.
14. Personal information is included only when it clarifies authorship, continuity, method, context, value, or intent.
15. Contact is an open channel rather than a conversion funnel.
16. The approved spatial identity is a quiet **Archive House** within the architectural metaverse.
17. A maintained Now layer distinguishes the present from the stable biography.
18. The district is structured to evolve from person to personal practice, studio, and company without erasing original authorship.
19. Final biography copy, specific values, personal records, portrait selection, and public contact details remain future content decisions.
20. This document governs future design and implementation of the About Universe.

# 32. Final Definition

The About Universe is the interpretive and human layer of the Personal Platform.

It reveals the author without making the platform self-centered. It organizes biography, experience, education, values, methods, languages, location, availability, credentials, collaborators, current activity, and contact into a coherent account of an evolving practice.

It enables professional decision-makers to evaluate relevance quickly while allowing other visitors to explore the deeper continuity between disciplines, places, questions, and ambitions.

As the practice expands, About becomes the bridge between the original individual author and a future studio or company.

> **About reveals who is responsible, how the practice became coherent, what guides it, and where it intends to go.**
