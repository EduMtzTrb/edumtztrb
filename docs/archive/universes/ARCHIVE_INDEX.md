# Universe Documents Archive — Index

## Purpose

This archive preserves the full history of the Personal Platform's universe specification documents: superseded approved versions, editorial review drafts, and merge history. Nothing here is current or authoritative. It exists so the reasoning behind Version 1.1 — what changed, why, and what it replaced — remains traceable rather than being deleted once a newer version was approved.

The current authoritative specification set lives outside this archive, in `docs/universes/`. See `docs/universes/README.md`.

## Merge history

Located at `docs/archive/universes/merge-history/`. The seven files here are the exact pre-approval Version 1.1 drafts — the final record of what was reviewed and corrected before approval, distinct from the approved copies now in `docs/universes/`. `UNIVERSE_V1.1_MERGE_REPORT.md` records the full merge and correction history across all passes: the initial merge from v1.0 and the review drafts, the terminology and ownership corrections, and the Research/Art/About factual corrections.

- `HOME_UNIVERSE_v1.1_DRAFT.md`
- `WORK_UNIVERSE_v1.1_DRAFT.md`
- `RESEARCH_UNIVERSE_v1.1_DRAFT.md`
- `LAB_UNIVERSE_v1.1_DRAFT.md`
- `ART_UNIVERSE_v1.1_DRAFT.md`
- `PRODUCTS_UNIVERSE_v1.1_DRAFT.md`
- `ABOUT_UNIVERSE_v1.1_DRAFT.md`
- `UNIVERSE_V1.1_MERGE_REPORT.md`

Each archived draft still carries its original pre-approval status block (`Status: Proposed Version 1.1`, `Authority: Non-authoritative until reviewed and approved`) — it was not overwritten with the approved metadata. The approved copy and the archived draft are deliberately distinct historical records: one shows what was approved, the other shows what was approved *from*.
