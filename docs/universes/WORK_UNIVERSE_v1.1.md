## 1. Definition

**Work demonstrates capability.** *(v1.1 coordination — see Merge Note.)*

The Work Universe is the professional face of the platform — the most resolved, accountable, and legible expressions of the practice, where architecture, BIM, computational design, AI, and software are read as applications of one architectural intelligence rather than separate careers.

It is not a portfolio gallery, an expanded résumé, or an indiscriminate archive. It lets a visitor understand what was designed, developed, delivered, or achieved; how the practice operates across disciplines; what judgment the author actually contributed; and where to look for deeper evidence.

> Work is where the practice becomes accountable.

---

## 2. Governing Concept — The Constructed Archive

An archive that stays active rather than nostalgic: **constructed**, because it is precise, deliberate, and structurally resolved; **archive**, because it holds an expanding body of work across years without becoming a static museum or a chronological dump. New work changes the foreground. Older work keeps its context. Different readings surface different continuities.

The experience is not browsing a folder of projects. It is moving through a body of evidence.

---

## 3. Purpose

Work performs five functions at once: establishes credibility for employers, collaborators, and peers; reveals one coherent practice across architecture, BIM, computation, AI, and software; supports rapid evaluation for a visitor with three minutes; rewards deeper exploration with method, recurrence, and change over time; and stays coherent as it grows from a personal portfolio into the face of an independent practice or studio.

---

## 4. Position Within the Platform

Home is the shared origin and the threshold into Work. Crossing that threshold increases spatial definition: stronger alignments, denser evidence, more deliberate rhythm.

| District | Owns |
|---|---|
| Research | structured questions, methods, theory, findings |
| Lab | experiments, prototypes, tests, unfinished systems |
| Art | expressive, perceptual, or interpretive intention |
| Products | the offer — acquisition, use, licensing, commissioning |
| About | the person, trajectory, values, and context |

A single initiative may touch several districts. It has exactly one canonical home. Work may reference the others without duplicating their content.

---

## 5. Entering Work

Not a portal, a gradual increase in definition: alignments tighten, frames gain substance, rhythm regularizes, evidence replaces suggestion.

**First impression:** *This is a serious, authored body of practice, and I can understand it without losing the possibility of discovery.*

**The threshold must provide**, without requiring the visitor to decode anything first: confirmation of arrival, a concise statement of what the district contains, visible routes to Selected Work and All Work, access to the practice areas, and continuity with Home.

---

## 6. Atmosphere

Work should feel rigorous, calm, exact, evidential, and quietly distinctive. It should not feel corporate, promotional, crowded, or aggressively technological — the four failure modes that actually matter, because each corresponds to a real temptation: corporate anonymity when trying to look serious, promotional inflation when trying to impress, crowding when trying to prove volume, and technological spectacle when trying to look capable.

The environment sits somewhere between an architectural archive, a drawing room, and a sequence of viewing bays — references that guide composition, not literal scenery.

Silence remains the baseline inherited from Home. Work is denser and more focused than Home. It is never noisy.

---

## 7. Identity

Work represents **applied architectural intelligence** — the same practice organizing physical space, coordinating building information, constructing computational systems, and designing software, without depending on any single job title, medium, or employer to hold it together.

**What the identity prioritizes:** evidence before promotion, contribution before association, judgment before software proficiency, coherence before variety, significance before volume.

---

## 8. Work and Lab: The Governing Distinction

The distinction is about the state and purpose of the output — never the technology used. AI, code, prototypes, and computation appear in either district.

- **Work asks:** What was designed, developed, delivered, or achieved?
- **Lab asks:** What is being tested, and what is being learned?

A project belongs in Work when its dominant public meaning is outcome and contribution. It belongs in Lab when its dominant public meaning is the experiment and the learning.

An initiative may begin in Lab and mature into a Work entry — the Lab record stays an experiment; the Work entry presents the resolved outcome; the two are linked (`Developed From`, `Tested Through`, `Resulted In`) and never duplicated.

**Work must not absorb every experiment merely because it looks impressive.** Doing so erases what Lab is for.

---

## 9. Practice Areas

Approved: **Architecture, Computational Design, AI and Software, Interdisciplinary Practice.**

BIM is a major capability and recurring mode of work, not a separate universe — it surfaces through whichever practice area actually describes the contribution, never treated as mere software proficiency.

Practice areas are **lenses**, not separate portfolios: one atmosphere, one navigational logic, one evidential standard, one archive. Cross-disciplinary work gets one canonical entry, one primary practice area, and additional classifications — forced into a single-discipline narrative only when that narrative is actually true.

---

## 10. Homepage

The homepage is the primary professional landing page — not a complete archive, and not required to display every project. It must answer, without long explanatory text: what kind of practice is this; what does it produce; which work best represents it now; how do I browse by relevance or discipline; how do I reach the complete archive.

**Sequence:** Threshold → Orientation Hall → Selected Field → Practice Crossings → Archive Depth → Crossroads.

- **Threshold** confirms location without delaying access.
- **Orientation Hall** establishes identity, breadth, and direct routes — orients, doesn't advertise.
- **Selected Field** is a curated, editable reading of the work that currently best represents the practice — not a permanent award category, not necessarily the newest work.
- **Practice Crossings** shows the archive reinterpreted through practice areas — connection, not division.
- **Archive Depth** signals clearly that Selected Field isn't everything, with direct passage to All Work and Collections.
- **Crossroads** reconnects to Research, Lab, About, Products (when relevant), and Home — a set of real relationships, not a footer or a recommendation carousel.

**Composition** uses a variable architectural field, not a uniform grid: prominence is editorial, never chronological by default and never a function of image size or client status. Empty space separates ideas. Alignment holds varied content together.

---

## 11. Navigation Model

Overview → Selected Work → All Work → Practice Areas → Collections → Work Entries.

- **Selected Work** is an editable curatorial state, not a permanent ranking.
- **All Work** prioritizes completeness and scanability over atmosphere.
- **Collections** reveal a theme, method, sector, or professional narrative without replacing canonical project identity or becoming arbitrary tags.

Exploration and direct access remain equally legitimate. Current location stays visible. No essential route depends on hover or spatial interpretation.

---

## 12. Browsing Years of Work

**Dual reading:** curated (Overview, Selected Work, Collections) and comprehensive (All Work, practice areas, chronology, search, Index).

**Time is context, not identity.** Years and periods reveal trajectory; they don't govern canonical routes or dominate the first reading. As volume grows, filters may add year, practice area, discipline, status, role, or collaborator — only when each one genuinely improves retrieval, never as a filtering demonstration for its own sake.

**Growth rule:** new work deepens the archive. It does not simply lengthen the homepage.

---

## 13. Project Presentation

This document defines how work is presented, not what any individual project says. Each substantial entry should make legible: identity, context, intent, personal role, contribution, method, outcome, credits, and relationships to Research, Lab, or Products.

**Evidence hierarchy:** built outcomes, drawings, models, diagrams, interfaces, process evidence, measurable results, and verified responsibilities — supporting claims, not replacing them with visual polish.

**Contribution transparency is non-negotiable.** Collaborative work must distinguish the total project from the author's role, the team's authorship, and the client's context. Work must never imply sole authorship where none existed.

Entries may use Feature, Standard, or Index depth depending on significance and available evidence — depth changes the amount of information shown, not the professional standard applied.

---

## 14. Layout and Media

Layouts are constructed from frames, datums, and controlled voids — inheriting architectural editorial qualities (measured alignment, sectional rhythm, framed evidence) without imitating a printed portfolio spread.

Density increases from homepage (low) through Selected Work (editorial) to All Work (functional). The district supports landscape and portrait images, drawings, diagrams, documents, video, interfaces, and mixed-media sequences — no universal crop or identical card format governs how work appears.

---

## 15. Interaction, Responsive, and Accessibility

Interaction reinforces orientation: entering deeper archive levels, changing a practice-area lens, comparing related work, preserving place on return. It never delays access to project information, turns browsing into a game, or makes essential information hover-dependent.

On small screens, Work becomes a **vertical architectural promenade** — clear orientation, selected-work hierarchy, and meaningful variation preserved, not collapsed into a repetitive card stack.

Atmosphere may enrich Work. It may never reduce access to professional evidence: semantic hierarchy, keyboard and touch access, reduced motion, readable contrast, and captions/credits where required all remain non-negotiable. A visitor must be able to understand the district without experiencing its atmospheric layer at all.

---

## 16. Future Scalability

A small early archive relies on Overview and Selected Work — the spatial framework stays credible without inventing volume to fill it. As work accumulates, All Work, practice areas, and Collections carry more weight while the homepage stays edited, never exhaustive. A mature archive may add deeper classification, advanced search, and role- or sector-based views — extensions of the archive layer, not redesigns of the identity.

A genuinely new discipline integrates through classification and an approved practice-area revision — never through a separate visual system or microsite.

**Growth toward a studio or company** must support expanded credits, collaborators, services, and organizational roles while preserving personal authorship. It must not invent a corporate identity before a real organization exists to justify one.

---

## 17. Empty and Transitional States

Before content is ready: honest neutral states, never invented projects, clients, or metrics. While media loads: frames hold their proportions; the composition never jumps. Sparse filtered views adapt their rhythm rather than appearing broken. Archived or unavailable work changes visibility explicitly — a removed entry never silently breaks a relationship or an external reference.

---

## 18. What Work Must Avoid

A generic project-card portfolio. A résumé in visual form. A chronological dump. A software-skills catalogue. An architecture-only identity hiding other mature work. A corporate agency site without personal authorship. An experimental playground indistinguishable from Lab. A complex database that prioritizes filtering over understanding.

Unchanged from v1.0. This document owns Work's experiential and spatial identity, the Work–Lab distinction, the homepage narrative, and the archive model. It does not own project selection, project copy, or any visual token. Changes to the governing concept, district boundary, or Work–Lab distinction require a formal revision of `WORK_UNIVERSE.md`.

## 21. Final Definition

The Work Universe is the constructed archive of the practice — calm, rigorous, and evolving, where architecture, BIM, computational design, AI, software, and interdisciplinary outcomes read as applications of one architectural intelligence. It does not prove versatility through accumulation. It demonstrates coherence through judgment.

**The Work Universe is where the platform earns professional trust.**
