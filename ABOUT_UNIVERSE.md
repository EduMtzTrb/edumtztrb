# ABOUT_UNIVERSE.md

## Personal Platform — About Universe

**Status:** Proposal  
**Domain:** About Universe  
**Scope:** Framework only  
**Excludes:** Final biography copy, complete career history, personal photographs, project case studies, contact details, implementation specifications

---

# 1. Purpose

The About Universe is the district where the visitor encounters the person, values, experiences, and evolving practice behind the platform.

It should not function as a conventional profile page, résumé, or autobiographical archive.

Its purpose is to answer:

- Who is behind this universe?
- What connects the different disciplines?
- How did this practice develop?
- What principles guide the work?
- What kinds of conversations, collaborations, and opportunities are relevant?

The About Universe should make the wider platform more legible by revealing the continuity between architecture, technology, research, art, experimentation, and product-making.

It transforms a potentially fragmented multidisciplinary profile into a coherent personal trajectory.

---

# 2. Core Concept

## The Inhabitant of the Universe

The Home Universe introduces the world.

The other universes reveal the work produced within it.

The About Universe reveals its inhabitant.

The visitor should not feel that they have left the architectural metaverse to read a biography. They should feel that they have entered a quieter, more intimate district containing traces of the person who designed the whole system.

The experience should resemble entering:

- an archive
- a studio
- a personal observatory
- a room of references
- a measured sequence of memories and intentions

The person should be understood through relationships, transitions, decisions, and recurring concerns rather than through self-promotion.

---

# 3. Strategic Role

The About Universe has five primary roles.

## 3.1 Humanize the platform

It gives a human presence to an otherwise spatial and systemic digital environment.

## 3.2 Create coherence

It explains why apparently different fields belong to one design practice.

## 3.3 Establish credibility

It presents experience, education, capabilities, and professional development without reproducing a standard résumé page.

## 3.4 Communicate intent

It makes values, motivations, and long-term direction visible.

## 3.5 Enable contact

It provides a calm and deliberate transition from observation to conversation.

---

# 4. Experience Principle

## Encounter, not introduction

The About Universe should avoid the typical structure:

> portrait → short bio → résumé → contact form

Instead, the visitor gradually constructs an understanding of the person through a sequence of spatial and informational encounters.

The experience should move from broad identity to specific evidence:

1. Presence
2. Personal position
3. Recurring values
4. Development over time
5. Experience and capabilities
6. Current direction
7. Invitation to contact

This creates depth without requiring excessive text at the entrance.

---

# 5. Atmosphere

The About Universe should feel:

- intimate
- reflective
- precise
- quiet
- open
- grounded
- personal without becoming confessional
- professional without becoming corporate

Compared with the other universes, it should have the smallest perceived scale.

The Home may feel infrastructural or monumental.

The Work Universe may feel ordered and productive.

The Research Universe may feel analytical.

The Lab may feel unstable and experimental.

The Art Universe may feel contemplative.

The About Universe should feel inhabited.

---

# 6. Architectural Metaphor

## The Archive House

The About Universe is conceived as an archive house inside the larger metaverse.

It contains a sequence of interconnected spaces:

- threshold
- portrait chamber
- biography corridor
- timeline gallery
- values room
- experience archive
- current desk
- contact aperture

These are conceptual spatial roles, not literal interface requirements.

The architecture should communicate proximity and accumulated time.

Recommended spatial characteristics:

- narrower passages
- lower visual density
- framed views
- layered translucent surfaces
- traces and annotations
- measured repetition
- subtle material warmth
- slower transitions
- occasional moments of stillness

The district should feel less public than the rest of the platform, but never closed.

# 7. Homepage Framework

The About homepage should operate as both a complete introduction and an entrance to deeper sections.

## 7.1 Threshold

The visitor enters through a restrained opening statement.

This area should include:

- name or personal identifier
- concise professional position
- one sentence connecting the disciplines
- a visual or spatial sign of personal presence
- an indication that the page can be explored beyond the initial viewport

The threshold should not contain a full biography.

Its role is orientation.

## 7.2 Identity Field

A composition that introduces the practice as a constellation rather than a job title.

Possible elements:

- primary disciplines
- recurring questions
- modes of practice
- places, contexts, or scales of work
- current areas of attention

These elements should appear relationally rather than as a skills list.

## 7.3 Story Preview

A short passage introduces the logic of the personal trajectory.

It should answer:

- where the practice began
- what caused it to expand
- what now connects its different directions

This is a preview leading toward the complete biography or timeline.

## 7.4 Selected Timeline

A small number of pivotal transitions are shown.

The homepage timeline should emphasize transformations rather than every date.

Examples of milestone types:

- first discipline
- major relocation
- important professional transition
- expansion into computation
- development of research interests
- emergence of an independent practice

## 7.5 Values Preview

Three to five core principles appear as a compact spatial sequence.

Each value should be expressed through:

- a title
- a concise statement
- an observable consequence in the work

## 7.6 Experience Overview

A high-level view of professional and educational experience.

It should present:

- fields of experience
- environments worked within
- scales of responsibility
- forms of collaboration
- selected capabilities

Detailed chronology remains secondary.

## 7.7 Current Position

A living section communicating the present moment.

It may include:

- current focus
- active questions
- availability status
- types of collaboration being considered
- geographic or professional context

This section must be easy to update without redesigning the page.

## 7.8 Contact Threshold

The final area should not feel like a conversion funnel.

It should present contact as an invitation to open a relevant conversation.

# 8. Personal Story Framework

## 8.1 Narrative Objective

The personal story should not attempt to narrate an entire life.

It should explain the evolution of the design practice.

The central narrative should connect:

- architectural formation
- technical and professional experience
- computational exploration
- research development
- artistic practice
- independent initiatives
- future direction

The story should emphasize continuity.

## 8.2 Narrative Structure

### Origin

What first established the relationship with design, space, making, or systems?

### Formation

What disciplines, environments, and methods shaped the foundation?

### Expansion

What made architecture extend into computation, software, AI, research, or art?

### Convergence

When did these areas begin to operate as one practice rather than separate interests?

### Present

What is the practice now trying to understand, build, or contribute?

### Open Future

Which directions remain deliberately unresolved?

## 8.3 Editorial Rules

The story should:

- use first-person voice
- remain specific
- avoid inflated claims
- explain transitions rather than list achievements
- acknowledge uncertainty and evolution
- maintain a balance between professional clarity and personal reflection

The story should not:

- reproduce a cover letter
- repeat project descriptions
- present every career event
- rely on generic statements about passion
- overexplain personal history
- treat multidisciplinary work as a collection of unrelated interests

# 9. Biography Framework

The biography should exist in several resolutions.

## 9.1 Micro Biography

One or two sentences.

Used in:

- About threshold
- metadata
- external profiles
- compact introductions
- search results

## 9.2 Short Biography

One concise paragraph.

Used in:

- About homepage
- speaking introductions
- selected professional contexts
- collaboration pages

## 9.3 Extended Biography

A structured narrative.

Used in:

- dedicated biography view
- press or institutional contexts
- detailed visitor exploration

## 9.4 Biography Components

The biography system should be built from reusable components:

- identity
- disciplinary foundation
- professional context
- methods
- recurring interests
- current focus
- future direction

This permits each version to remain consistent without being identical.

## 9.5 Biography Tone

The biography should be:

- factual
- reflective
- direct
- internationally legible
- free of unnecessary jargon
- confident without sounding promotional

---

# 10. Timeline Framework

## 10.1 Purpose

The timeline should visualize development, not merely chronology.

It should show how experiences, disciplines, and interests accumulated and intersected.

## 10.2 Timeline Model

A multi-track timeline is preferable to a single résumé line.

Potential tracks:

- education
- professional practice
- research
- technology
- independent work
- art and creative practice
- geographic movement
- major conceptual shifts

The tracks may converge, split, overlap, or temporarily disappear.

## 10.3 Event Hierarchy

Timeline events should have three levels.

### Pivotal events

Moments that changed the direction of the practice.

### Development events

Periods where a capability, discipline, or role deepened.

### Context events

Supporting facts that provide orientation but do not require prominence.

## 10.4 Interaction Principles

The timeline should support:

- overview before detail
- gradual disclosure
- filtering by track
- connections between related events
- optional links to relevant universes
- clear reading without interaction

The timeline must remain understandable on small screens.

## 10.5 Visual Language

Recommended visual references:

- architectural section lines
- survey marks
- measured intervals
- layered paths
- drawing revisions
- traces of movement
- overlapping construction phases

The timeline should not resemble a corporate career infographic.

---

# 11. Values Framework

## 11.1 Purpose

Values should communicate how decisions are made.

They should not appear as abstract moral branding.

Each value must connect to observable behavior, design choices, or working methods.

## 11.2 Value Structure

Each value should include:

1. Principle
2. Interpretation
3. Practical consequence
4. Tension or limit

This prevents values from becoming slogans.

## 11.3 Potential Value Territories

The final values remain to be approved, but the framework should accommodate themes such as:

- openness
- rigor
- curiosity
- accessibility
- coherence
- adaptability
- care
- independence
- experimentation
- responsibility
- precision
- long-term thinking

## 11.4 Presentation

Values should be encountered one at a time or in small groups.

They may appear as:

- spatial inscriptions
- annotated fragments
- paired statements
- principles embedded in a conceptual plan
- a sequence of quiet rooms

Avoid a grid of identical cards.

---

# 12. Experience Framework

## 12.1 Purpose

The experience section should establish credibility while preserving the identity of the About Universe.

It should show what kinds of environments, responsibilities, and problems have shaped the practice.

## 12.2 Experience Categories

The framework should support:

- professional roles
- education
- collaborations
- teaching or mentoring
- exhibitions
- publications
- talks
- awards
- residencies
- certifications
- independent ventures

Not all categories need to be visible at launch.

## 12.3 Experience Entry

Each entry may include:

- organization or context
- role
- period
- location
- responsibility
- contribution
- methods or tools
- relationship to the wider practice
- related universe or project

## 12.4 Reading Modes

Two reading modes should coexist.

### Narrative mode

Shows how experience contributed to the development of the practice.

### Reference mode

Provides concise, scannable professional facts.

The user should not need to download a résumé to understand the main trajectory.

A downloadable résumé may exist as a secondary utility, but should not replace the experience framework.

## 12.5 Capability Constellation

Capabilities should be presented as relationships between disciplines and methods.

Examples of groupings:

- spatial design
- technical coordination
- computational systems
- visual communication
- research
- prototyping
- product thinking

Avoid long software inventories as the principal representation of competence.

---

# 13. Current Practice Framework

The About Universe should contain a clearly maintained representation of the present.

This prevents the biography from becoming static.

## 13.1 Current State Components

- current role or position
- current location or operating context
- current research
- active initiatives
- areas being developed
- collaboration interests
- availability
- recent transition or focus

## 13.2 Temporal Labels

Content should be explicitly distinguished as:

- past
- ongoing
- current
- emerging
- paused
- completed

This is especially important as the platform grows.

## 13.3 Update Rhythm

The current-practice layer should be designed for periodic revision without modifying the stable biography or timeline.

---

# 14. Contact Experience

## 14.1 Principle

Contact should feel like opening a door, not submitting a lead.

The experience must be direct, respectful, and low-friction.

## 14.2 Contact Intentions

Visitors may contact for different reasons:

- professional opportunities
- collaborations
- research
- commissions
- exhibitions
- products
- speaking
- teaching
- press
- general conversation

The interface may help visitors identify the context without forcing them through a complex form.

## 14.3 Contact Sequence

### Invitation

A concise statement clarifies which conversations are welcome.

### Context selection

The visitor may optionally select the nature of the message.

### Message

A minimal contact method.

### Alternative channels

Relevant external or professional platforms may be provided.

### Response expectation

The interface may communicate expected response behavior without promising an exact timeline.

## 14.4 Contact Modes

The system should support:

- direct email
- minimal contact form
- selected professional networks
- optional calendar or meeting request in the future
- location or timezone context when relevant

## 14.5 Form Principles

The contact form should request only essential information.

Recommended fields:

- name
- contact address
- reason for contact
- message

Optional contextual fields may appear dynamically.

Avoid:

- excessive qualification
- mandatory phone numbers
- marketing consent patterns
- sales-oriented language
- decorative form complexity

## 14.6 Contact Confirmation

The confirmation state should remain inside the universe.

It may resemble:

- a transmitted signal
- an opened channel
- a marked drawing
- a light appearing in the archive

It should clearly confirm success without theatrical delay.

# 15. Visual Identity

## 15.1 Relationship to the Shared System

The About Universe must use the platform’s shared:

- typography
- spacing logic
- grid
- navigation conventions
- interaction rules
- accessibility standards
- core architectural line language

Its identity should emerge through atmosphere and composition, not through an unrelated visual system.

## 15.2 Distinctive Characteristics

The About Universe may introduce:

- warmer tonal values
- smaller-scale spatial compositions
- finer line weights
- handwritten or annotated secondary marks
- archival labels
- translucent layers
- quiet image framing
- subtle signs of use or inhabitation
- denser relationships between text and trace

## 15.3 Portrait Strategy

Portraiture should be optional and deliberate.

Possible approaches:

- a restrained photographic portrait
- silhouette
- partial view
- spatial portrait inside a studio or environment
- abstract personal trace
- moving image used sparingly
- no portrait, with presence communicated through objects and text

A conventional centered headshot should not become the defining image unless intentionally selected.

## 15.4 Personal Materials

The visual framework may accommodate:

- sketches
- notebook fragments
- diagrams
- annotated plans
- photographs of places
- tools
- books
- material studies
- personal archives
- incomplete thoughts

These should reveal ways of thinking rather than function as decoration.

## 15.5 Color and Light

Compared with the more neutral or infrastructural Home Universe, the About Universe may use slightly warmer light.

The distinction should remain subtle.

Potential qualities:

- paper
- graphite
- warm white
- muted stone
- soft shadow
- subdued metallic reflection
- low-saturation personal accents

Final colors remain governed by the shared Design System.

# 16. Layout System

## 16.1 Spatial Rhythm

Layouts should alternate between:

- compressed biographical passages
- open fields
- narrow chronological bands
- framed personal evidence
- isolated statements
- layered archival compositions

This creates a slower and more reflective pace.

## 16.2 Text Width

Biography text should use controlled line lengths.

Long passages should be divided into:

- chapters
- fragments
- periods
- questions
- transitions

Avoid one continuous autobiographical wall of text.

## 16.3 Image Relationships

Images should not merely illustrate adjacent text.

They may operate as:

- evidence
- memory
- context
- interruption
- atmosphere
- temporal marker

Captions should clarify their role.

## 16.4 Modular Zones

The framework should support reusable zones:

- identity zone
- narrative zone
- timeline zone
- values zone
- experience zone
- current-state zone
- contact zone

Their order may adapt across screen sizes while preserving narrative logic.

---

# 17. Navigation

## 17.1 Internal Navigation

Primary internal destinations:

- Story
- Timeline
- Values
- Experience
- Now
- Contact

These may function as sections, views, or spatial locations.

## 17.2 Global Navigation Relationship

The About Universe must retain clear access to all other districts.

It should also provide contextual links from biography and experience to:

- Work
- Research
- Lab
- Art
- Products

These connections must be selective.

The About Universe should explain relationships between universes, not duplicate their content.

## 17.3 Return Paths

Visitors arriving through a project or external link should easily understand:

- where they are
- who the person is
- how to return to the originating universe
- how to continue toward contact

---

# 18. Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel attentive rather than performative.

Recommended interaction qualities:

- slow reveals
- line traces
- layered transitions
- subtle parallax
- timeline unfolding
- annotations appearing on focus
- connected events highlighting together
- restrained cursor responses
- state changes based on proximity or reading progress

Avoid:

- exaggerated personality animations
- novelty biography interactions
- excessive scrolling choreography
- hidden essential information
- interactions that make professional facts difficult to retrieve

All essential content must remain accessible without motion.

---

# 19. Responsive Experience

## Desktop

The spatial metaphor may be most fully expressed.

Possible characteristics:

- multiple concurrent timeline tracks
- layered image and text compositions
- visible relationships between sections
- subtle environmental transitions

## Tablet

The experience should preserve sequence and layering while reducing simultaneous information.

## Mobile

The mobile experience should become a clear personal narrative.

Priorities:

- strong opening identity
- readable biography
- linear timeline
- concise values
- accessible experience records
- immediate contact route

Mobile should not attempt to simulate a compressed desktop environment.

---

# 20. Accessibility

The About Universe contains personal and professional information and must prioritize clarity.

Requirements include:

- semantic biography structure
- clear heading hierarchy
- readable text sizes
- sufficient contrast
- keyboard-accessible timeline controls
- reduced-motion mode
- descriptive image alternatives
- captions for personal archive material
- non-visual alternatives for spatial diagrams
- contact forms with explicit labels and errors
- no essential information communicated only through position, color, or animation

The timeline must have a fully readable list-based representation.

      ↓

# 27. Anti-Patterns

The About Universe should avoid:

- a conventional résumé page
- a giant portrait followed by generic copy
- a list of software logos
- excessive personal branding language
- autobiography without relevance to the practice
- unexplained career chronology
- a skills-percentage chart
- a social-media wall
- awards used as the primary identity
- hidden contact details
- long unstructured text
- sentimental visual clichés
- artificial intimacy
- forced informality
- a contact form designed like a sales funnel

# 30. Approval Questions

Before moving toward detailed content or implementation, the following decisions should be approved:

1. Should the About Universe feel primarily like an archive, a studio, a home, or another spatial type?
2. How personal should the narrative become?
3. Should a recognizable portrait appear at the threshold?
4. Should the timeline expose parallel life and practice tracks?
5. Which values are foundational rather than aspirational?
6. How much professional detail should remain visible without opening a résumé?
7. Should the current-practice section include availability and active opportunities?
8. Which contact routes should be public?
9. Should personal archival material be used?
10. Should the About Universe support multiple languages from its first release?

---

# 31. Proposed Direction

The recommended direction is:

> A quiet archive house inside the wider architectural metaverse, where the visitor reconstructs the person and practice through biography, parallel timelines, values, professional evidence, personal traces, and an open channel for contact.

This direction preserves the platform’s architectural identity while allowing the About Universe to become more intimate, human, and temporally layered than the other districts.
