# Personal Platform — Project Instructions

## Project Overview

This project is dedicated to the complete design, development, and long-term evolution of my personal digital platform.

This is **not** simply a portfolio website.

The platform represents my complete professional and creative ecosystem, bringing together every discipline, project, product, and idea under a single coherent identity.

It should be designed as a long-term platform that can continuously grow for many years without requiring fundamental structural redesign.

---

# Primary Goal

Create a timeless, highly curated, immersive digital platform that serves simultaneously as:

- personal website
- architecture portfolio
- professional portfolio
- computational design portfolio
- AI portfolio
- research platform
- laboratory for experiments
- art gallery
- product showcase
- digital storefront
- future company platform

Every future activity should naturally integrate into the platform.

---

# Core Philosophy

The platform should not communicate:

> "I work in many unrelated fields."

Instead it should communicate:

> "Architecture, computational design, software, AI, research, and art are different expressions of the same design practice."

The platform should present a coherent personal universe rather than a collection of webpages.

---

# Design Vision

The website is conceived as an explorable architectural metaverse.

The visitor should feel like they are entering a carefully designed architectural world rather than browsing a conventional website.

The Home section is not merely a homepage.

It is the entrance to the universe.

Every major category becomes its own architectural district inside this shared world.

---

# Design Principles

The entire platform should follow these principles.

## Timeless

Avoid trendy visual styles.

The platform should remain visually relevant for many years.

---

## Minimal

Every element should have purpose.

Remove unnecessary decoration.

Prioritize clarity and composition.

---

## Architectural

The visual language should originate from architecture.

Examples include:

- line drawings
- construction geometry
- grids
- plans
- sections
- axonometric thinking
- spatial hierarchy
- proportion
- rhythm

---

## Atmospheric

The experience should feel calm.

Use space, light, shadow and scale instead of visual noise.

---

## Exploratory

Visitors should feel they are discovering spaces rather than navigating pages.

---

## Cohesive

Every section belongs to the same universe.

Each category develops its own identity without breaking the overall visual language.

---

# The Universe

The platform is organized as an architectural world.

The Home establishes the rules of this world.

Every major category becomes an independent district with its own atmosphere while remaining part of the same universe.

Future districts may be added without redesigning the platform.

---

# Current Major Districts

- Home
- Work
- Research
- Lab
- Art
- Products
- About

Additional districts may be introduced in future versions.

---

# Information Architecture Philosophy

The project should prioritize:

1. Structure before visuals.
2. Visual language before implementation.
3. Systems before individual pages.
4. Reusable components before custom solutions.
5. Scalability over short-term convenience.

---

# Development Philosophy

Development should proceed in clearly separated phases.

## Phase 1

Platform vision.

## Phase 2

Information architecture.

## Phase 3

Design system.

## Phase 4

Universe design.

## Phase 5

Category homepages.

## Phase 6

Shared components.

## Phase 7

Project content.

## Phase 8

Optimization and deployment.

No later phase should begin until the previous phase has been approved.

---

# Conversation Structure

This ChatGPT Project will be divided into multiple specialized conversations.

Each conversation owns a specific domain.

Everything approved inside that conversation becomes the source of truth for that domain.

Conversations should never silently redefine decisions made elsewhere.

Cross-domain changes must be explicitly identified and communicated.

---

# Decision Status

Every important decision should be classified as one of the following:

- Idea
- Proposal
- Approved
- Implemented
- Verified
- Deprecated

Never present assumptions as approved decisions.

---

# Source of Truth

Whenever appropriate, conversations should propose updates to the relevant project documentation.

Examples include:

- PROJECT_STATE.md
- ROADMAP.md
- BACKLOG.md
- DECISIONS.md
- CHANGELOG.md

and the documentation specific to each project area.

---

# Claude Code Integration

Claude Code is responsible for implementation.

It should not independently redefine project vision or design decisions.

Implementation should always follow the approved project documentation.

Claude Code tasks should be:

- small
- sequential
- testable
- independently verifiable

Large implementation requests should always be decomposed into smaller milestones.
